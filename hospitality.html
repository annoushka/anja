wellness hospitality & sustainability consulting

helping hotels create clearer, healthier, and more considerate stays for every guest.

i’m beginning to offer wellness hospitality and sustainability consulting for hotels and guest-focused spaces.

my interest comes from my own experience as a health-conscious, plant-based, and fragrance-sensitive traveler. i often notice how small details — unclear menu labels, limited plant-based options, strong scents in rooms — can make a stay feel less welcoming.

what i bring is a fresh guest perspective, a strong interest in wellbeing and sustainability, and a practical desire to help hotels make changes that feel thoughtful, visible, and possible.

what i can help with

i focus on practical guest-experience upgrades that can improve comfort, trust, and clarity for health-conscious, plant-based, and fragrance-sensitive travelers.

clearer menu labeling and plant-based options
plant milk available without a surcharge
clearer ingredient communication
unscented amenity options
more considerate room and sleep environments
simple waste-reduction and ethical-sourcing ideas
staff communication around common guest questions
guest-feedback review and prioritized improvements

the goal is to identify inexpensive, visible changes that can make guests feel more considered — and help a hotel communicate its values more clearly.

healthy stay audit

the healthy stay audit is a focused review of how a property currently supports guest wellbeing, clarity, and sustainability.

i review:

menus and breakfast options
booking-page information
room amenities and toiletries
sleep environment and sensory comfort
dietary communication
sustainability practices and waste-reduction opportunities
staff communication and guest feedback

you receive:

a concise report with 10–20 prioritized recommendations
a “quick wins” list for changes that can be made with minimal cost or disruption

how we work

we begin with a short conversation to understand your property, guest profile, dining setup, current amenities, and goals.

then i review the relevant guest journey — from booking and arrival to breakfast, the room, communication, and post-stay feedback.

i organize findings into clear categories such as nutrition, labeling, amenities, sensory comfort, sleep, accessibility, sustainability, and staff communication.

important boundaries

i provide advisory support, guest-experience review, and practical recommendations. your team remains responsible for implementing changes and for food safety, allergen claims, accessibility requirements, and local compliance.
